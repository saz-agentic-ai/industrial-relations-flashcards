Legal status, November 2026

The Trade Unions Act, 1926 and the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 have been replaced by the Industrial Relations Code, 2020, in force from 21 November 2025. Syllabi still teach the Acts, so each topic explains the Act first and flags what the Code changed.
